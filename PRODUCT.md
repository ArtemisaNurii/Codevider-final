# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B decision-makers — CTOs, VP Engineering, product leaders, and founders — evaluating whether to embed an external software team. They arrive with a concrete delivery problem (backlog pressure, missing expertise, slow time-to-market) and need proof that Codevider is a credible, low-risk partner, not a commodity body shop.

## Product Purpose

The Codevider website is a marketing surface that converts qualified B2B leads into project conversations. Success means visitors quickly understand what Codevider does, trust the team's expertise, see relevant proof (services, partnerships, delivery model), and reach contact with confidence. The homepage is the primary entry point and must establish expert credibility within the first scroll.

## Brand Personality

Confident · Expert · Reliable

Voice is direct and technically literate without jargon overload. The site should feel like a senior engineering partner who has shipped before — calm assurance, not hype. Motion and visuals demonstrate capability; copy earns trust through specificity.

## Anti-references

- Cheap outsourcing clichés: stock handshakes, "10x developer" claims, red urgency banners, rate-card-first positioning, anonymous offshore factory aesthetics
- Generic SaaS landing templates: cream backgrounds, hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, gradient text, glassmorphism decoration
- Flashy agency sites that prioritize spectacle over substance

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — Interactive demos, live-feeling UI mockups, and concrete service detail over abstract claims.
2. **Expert confidence** — Typography and layout carry authority; avoid salesy urgency or discount positioning.
3. **Proof before pitch** — Partnerships, delivery stats, and service depth appear before the contact CTA feels earned.
4. **Practice what you preach** — The site itself demonstrates the engineering quality Codevider sells (performance, accessibility, polish).
5. **Restrained motion** — Animation supports comprehension and delight; never gates content or signals cheap flash.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AAA where feasible across the marketing site. Priority areas: color contrast on body and muted text, keyboard navigation for all interactive elements, screen-reader semantics for section structure and demos, and full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for hero WebGL (PixelBlast), scroll reveals, and carousel/tab interactions.
